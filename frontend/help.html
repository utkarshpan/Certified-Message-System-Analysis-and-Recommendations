🚀 Getting Started
How do I create an account? ▼
1. Click "Sign Up" on the login page
2. Enter your name, email, and password (minimum 6 characters)
3. Click "Create Account"
4. You'll be redirected to the dashboard
How do I login? ▼
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Login"
Your session will remain active for 7 days with the JWT token.
✅ Message Validation
What is a message validation? ▼
Message validation is the process of checking if your message is likely to be true or false using AI analysis, rule-based checks, and real-time data verification. Our system uses Google Gemini AI to detect fake news patterns.
How does the AI validation work? ▼
Our AI uses Google Gemini to:
  • Detect fake news patterns and sensational language
  • Check for chain message characteristics
  • Analyze credibility based on specific details
  • Provide confidence scores (0-100%)
What is the weather verification? ▼
If your message mentions weather (rain, storm, temperature, etc.), our system uses real-time weather API to:
  • Get actual weather conditions for mentioned locations
  • Compare with claims in your message
  • Flag mismatches (e.g., claiming rain when it's clear)
💡 Tip:
Provide specific locations and times for accurate verification.
What does the credibility score mean? ▼
The credibility score ranges from 0-100%:
  • 75-100%: High confidence - Message appears credible
  • 60-74%: Medium confidence - Needs verification
  • Below 60%: Low confidence - Likely contains false information
✨ Features & Capabilities
What are the validation checks? ▼
Our system performs three checks:
  • Keyword Check: Looks for specific weather/alert keywords
  • Location Check: Verifies Indian cities/locations are mentioned
  • Time Check: Ensures specific time/date is included
Can I share validated messages? ▼
Yes! After validation, you can share your verified message to:
  • 🐦 Twitter
  • 💬 WhatsApp
  • 📘 Facebook
Your message will be shared with credibility information.
🔧 Troubleshooting
My validation is taking too long ▼
Validation typically takes 2-3 seconds. If it's taking longer:
  • Check your internet connection
  • Ensure the backend server is running
  • Try refreshing the page
  • Clear your browser cache
I got a validation error ▼
Common errors and solutions:
  • "Message required": Please enter a message before validating
  • Connection error: Check if backend is running (npm start)
  • Invalid token: Try logging out and logging back in
  • AI service unavailable: Fallback validation will be used
How do I view my validation history? ▼
Go to your Dashboard to see:
  • All messages you've validated
  • Validation results (passed/failed)
  • Confidence scores
  • Timestamps
Your message history is saved for 6 months.
💡 Best Practices
Tips for better validation results ▼
For best results:
  • ✅ Be specific - Use exact locations, times, and dates
  • ✅ Include context - Add relevant details and keywords
  • ✅ Avoid sensational language - Don't use ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation!!!
  • ✅ Fact-check claims - Provide verifiable information
  • ❌ Don't forward blindly - Validate before sharing
